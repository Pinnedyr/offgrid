07 / QUESTIONS

Quick answers.

Does OFFGRID disable exam mode?

No. OFFGRID is a browser start page. It doesn't disable exam mode, but it lets you access the whole internet from one file.

Can I access every website?

OFFGRID lets you enter website addresses and search the web. Whether a website actually opens depends on your browser and network.

What do I get?

A lightweight standalone HTML file. No installer, no complicated setup and no unnecessary nonsense.

Will my teacher know?

We don't provide invisibility cloaks. School-managed devices and networks may still have activity monitoring, and teachers have a sixth sense for exactly this. Make sure the teacher isn't looking. Then check again.

Is the Teacher Radar real?

No. It's a joke with a spinning thing on it. It detects absolutely nothing.

What if I'm still taking the test?

Then close this tab, answer question 4 and come back when you're done. The internet will wait. It always does.
